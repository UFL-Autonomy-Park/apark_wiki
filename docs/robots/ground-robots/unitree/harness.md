![A Unitree robot dog hanging by a strap from the crossbar of an aluminum frame, its feet just off the floor.](../../../images/ground-robots/unitree-harness.jpg){ width="40%" }

*The harness frame holding a robot dog (from the Unitree Harness Manual).*

The harness can be used with the Go1 or the B1 to test a low-level motor controller (for example, RL) on the dog in a safe way before having it work on its own. It has casters on the bottom of the frame to allow the robot to walk around while being safely restrained.

The metal needed to assemble the harness is in the Indoor Lab and the casters/brackets/fasteners for T-slot extrusion are at the Autonomy Park (some parts may also be in the annex down the hall from the indoor lab). The design outlined in the manual is very flimsy and the way the quadruped attaches to the frame is very haphazard. There is enough material to add reinforcement, which is reccommended. It also takes up a lot of space and needs a flat surface, which is why there is not one currently assembled.
