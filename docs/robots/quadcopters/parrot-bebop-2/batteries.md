Keep track of the hardware issues of the Bebops on this page and on [Known Issues](known-issues.md). See [Edit the Wiki](../../../start-here/edit-the-wiki.md).

None of the cycle counts or ages of the LiPos for the Parrot Bebops are known. The following advice is generally given.

!!! note
    Unfortunately, Parrot's proprietary chargers do not allow charging to storage voltages or even checking the battery voltage. This is why it's important to keep tabs on their swelling, as it's the primary sign of age.

!!! warning "Tell the Lab Manager About Old or Dead Batteries"
    Please tell the lab manager to pick up any old or nonfunctional batteries. A blinding red light on the charger means the battery is due for disposal. A short flight time is another sign. Do not keep using a battery that shows either one.
