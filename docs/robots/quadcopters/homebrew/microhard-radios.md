A wireless LAN can be problematic with many agents, and it is untested at present. Microhard radios are the way to go. We have one for each Homebrew. They connect to the Jetson on each Homebrew over USB.

## What Is Needed

Each radio needs an additional voltage regulator soldered to the PDB. This needs some disassembly of the quadcopter. Test the work on **one** Homebrew before doing more.
