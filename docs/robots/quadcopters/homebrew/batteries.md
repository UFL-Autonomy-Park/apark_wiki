The Homebrews fly on 6S LiPo batteries, which we call the Homebrew batteries. No one has counted how many we have. Some are at the Autonomy Park and some are at the Indoor Lab.

Most of them should have a plastic sheet on them to help keep track of the number of cycles. Please use it whenever you can, to help track battery age.

A Homebrew's battery level is voltage-only and sags under load: see [Known Issues](known-issues.md#battery-readings-are-voltage-only).
