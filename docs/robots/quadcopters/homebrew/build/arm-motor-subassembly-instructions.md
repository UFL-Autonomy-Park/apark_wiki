## Required Tools
- 2 mm hex screwdriver  
- 2.5 mm hex screwdriver

## Assembly Steps

### Step 1
Remove the **2 mm horizontal screw** that goes through the arm tube.  

![2](../../../../images/homebrew/arm-assembly/step-2.jpg){ width="50%" }
---

### Step 2
Slide in the plastic brackets that will mount the arm to the body plates later.  

![3](../../../../images/homebrew/arm-assembly/step-3.jpg){ width="50%" }
---

### Step 3
Place the motor on the motor mount (assemble according to airframe manual) and use **four 2.5 mm shoulder screws (same box with the motor)** to fasten them.  
Install all four screws before tightening them.  

![4](../../../../images/homebrew/arm-assembly/step-4.jpg){ width="50%" }
![5](../../../../images/homebrew/arm-assembly/step-5.jpg){ width="50%" }
---

### Step 4
Slide the motor mount onto the arm tube with the motor cable facing outward.  

![6](../../../../images/homebrew/arm-assembly/step-6.jpg){ width="50%" }
---

### Step 5
Tuck the cables into the arm and push them through to the other end.  
Place the horizontal screw back in position.  

![7](../../../../images/homebrew/arm-assembly/step-7.jpg){ width="50%" }
