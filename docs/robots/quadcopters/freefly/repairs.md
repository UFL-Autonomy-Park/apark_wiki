!!! warning "Repairs Take a Year or More"
    Freefly repairs of the Astros take about a year or more. See [Known Issues](astro/known-issues.md).

## Freefly Service Request

1. Submit a service request on [Freefly's website](https://freeflysystems.com/service-request).
2. In **User Details**, enter `University of Florida Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering Department` in the **Company** field.
3. For **Point of Purchase**, select **Other**.
4. Find the purchase date for the drone on the original invoices. They are in the Google Drive, in the folder `Freefly Systems`. Upload the appropriate invoice.
5. The serial number of each drone is in a Google Sheet in the base folder of the Drive.

!!! danger "Allow the Transfer in Auterion Suite"
    This is the most important step. Log in to Auterion Suite, select **Fleet Management**, then **Vehicles**. Select the drone that crashed or was damaged. Click the **Settings** gear, then **Allow Transfer**.

!!! warning "Ship the Remote Too"
    Do not forget to include the associated remote with the drone shipment.

## Packing

* **Astro:** use the Pelican case.
* **Alta:** use the foam we have in an appropriately sized cardboard box. The Taranis remote ships separately in its own case, with its own myMAE shipping request.

!!! danger "Do Not Throw This Foam Away!"
    The foam is needed to ship the Alta.
