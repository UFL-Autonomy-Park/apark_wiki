The Astro's USB-C port is on the underside of the quadcopter. A rubber dust cover hides it: remove the cover to see the port.

![The underside of an Astro with a finger pointing at the rubber dust cover, a panel with three slots near the right edge.](../../../../images/freefly/astro-usb-c-cover.jpg){ width="40%" }

*The dust cover (the finger points to it).*

![The underside of an Astro with the dust cover removed, showing the connector board and its USB-C port.](../../../../images/freefly/astro-usb-c-port.jpg){ width="40%" }

*The USB-C port, with the cover removed.*

## Open the Dashboard Over USB-C

While your computer is connected to this port, open `https://10.41.1.1/` in a web browser to reach the Astro's [dashboard](operate/dashboard.md). You can also change the Astro's [parameters](operate/parameters.md) there.
