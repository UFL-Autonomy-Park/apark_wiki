## Can I Borrow Hardware?

No. All hardware belongs to Dr. Kamalapurkar, and we do not lend it to anyone outside the SCC roster. See [Lab Rules](lab-rules.md).

## Which Lab Is Which?

The Autonomy Park is the outdoor space at the UF Energy Park, covered by a flight net. It is a university resource. The Indoor Lab is the motion-capture lab in MAE-A 313, which belongs to the SCC Lab. See [Lab Spaces and Dimensions](lab-spaces-and-dimensions.md).

## Do I Need Training?

Yes. Everyone must watch the safety video and pass the quiz with at least 80%. The Indoor Lab also needs two EHS trainings. See [Safety](safety.md).

## When Will I Get Access?

Expect one to two weeks. Access moves in two stages. Dr. Kamalapurkar has to approve it first. Then UF MAE Facilities has to input your door codes. If it takes longer than two weeks, email [facilities@mae.ufl.edu](mailto:facilities@mae.ufl.edu). See [Lab Access](access/lab-access.md).

## Where Do I Find the Wi-Fi Password or a Login?

The Wi-Fi password, the Autonomy Park Gmail login (and with it the Autonomy Park Google Chrome profile with all its useful bookmarks), and the logins to robots and computers cannot be published here, because they are sensitive. Ask one of the people on the [Contacts](contacts.md) page.

## How Do I Schedule Time at the Autonomy Park?

Email one of the people on the [Contacts](contacts.md) page. See [Schedule a Time](schedule-a-time.md).

## Can I Fly in the Rain?

No. Do not fly in rain, storms, high wind, or other bad weather. See [Lab Rules](lab-rules.md).

## Where Is the Autonomy Park Bathroom?

It is due west of the Autonomy Park, on the north side of the Student Design building. Look for the door with a keypad. If you are near the garage door, you are at the wrong door.
