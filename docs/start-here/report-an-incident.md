## Equipment Crash

If only equipment is involved (for example, a quadcopter crashes inside the net and the landing gear breaks, but no one is hurt), report it to Dr. Kamalapurkar.

## Injury

Do these in order:

1. **Get medical attention immediately.** For a serious or life-threatening injury, go to the emergency room or call 911, especially if you are alone. For a minor injury, go to urgent care.
2. **Tell Dr. Kamalapurkar.**
3. **Submit an incident report with UF Environmental Health and Safety (EHS).**
4. **Call AmeriSys at 1-800-455-2079 if you are employed by the University** (for example, a graduate student researcher with a stipend). This is so you can get paid later if your injury causes disability or lifelong problems.

[Submit an EHS Incident Report](https://apps.ehs.ufl.edu/incidents){ .md-button }

## LiPo Fire

Get away and call 911. Do not try to fight it. Never use an extinguisher, water, or sand. See [Lab Rules](lab-rules.md#lipo-batteries).

## After a Crash

Never enter the net while a quadcopter is armed. See [Lab Rules](lab-rules.md#flying).
