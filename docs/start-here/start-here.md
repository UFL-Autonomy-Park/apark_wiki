There are two places. The **Autonomy Park** is the outdoor flight net at the UF Energy Park, a university resource. The **Indoor Lab** is the motion-capture lab in MAE-A 313, which belongs to the SCC Lab.

## Do These in Order

1. **View our lab spaces.** [Lab Spaces and Dimensions](lab-spaces-and-dimensions.md) helps you see if either of our spaces are right for your experiment. We do not lend hardware.
2. **Know who to ask.** [Contacts](contacts.md) lists the PI and the lab managers.
3. **Finish the safety training.** Watch the video and pass the quiz: [Safety](safety.md).
4. **Read the rules.** [Lab Rules](lab-rules.md) is short. Read it all.
5. **Request access.** [Lab Access](access/lab-access.md) for the Autonomy Park and the Indoor Lab. It takes up to two weeks.
6. **Book a time.** [Schedule a Time](schedule-a-time.md) to use the Autonomy Park since it's usually in active use.
