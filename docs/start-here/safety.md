## EHS Trainings: Indoor Lab Only

If you need the **Indoor Lab**, you must also complete two UF Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) trainings. The Autonomy Park does not need them.

| Training | Why It Is Required | How Often |
|---|---|---|
| EHS 809: Hazardous Waste Management | Required for all UF labs | Repeat every 365 days|
| EHS 869: Chemical Hygiene Plan | The Indoor Lab has chemicals (isopropyl alcohol and hot glue) | Once, ever |

To take either training:

1. Go to [UF MyTraining](https://mytraining.hr.ufl.edu/).
2. Click **Continue with GatorLink** and sign in if prompted.
3. In the search bar (top right corner), search for the course number (`EHS809` or `EHS869`).
4. There should be one result: **Hazardous Waste Management** (EHS 809) or **Chemical Hygiene Plan** (EHS 869). Click on it.
5. Click **Start**. Once you start, you can always resume later.
6. Allow pop-ups, or the training will not open.

[UF MyTraining](https://mytraining.hr.ufl.edu/){ .md-button }
