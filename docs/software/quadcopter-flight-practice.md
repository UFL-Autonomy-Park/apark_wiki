Liftoff: FPV Drone Racing is a good simulation of flying a quadcopter by hand. It is the best place to practice before you fly one of our robots, because a crash costs nothing.

## What You Need

- An RC controller, such as our Taranis X9D (with a USB-B cable). Liftoff only works with a game or radio controller, not a keyboard.
- A computer: Mac, Windows, or Linux.
- A Steam account. The Steam login for the lab is in the Autonomy Park Bitwarden. If you need access, see [Contacts](../start-here/contacts.md).

## How to Connect the Controller

The Taranis X9D connects to the computer over USB. You need a USB-B cable.
