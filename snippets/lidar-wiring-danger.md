!!! danger "Never Power On a Wrongly Wired Lidar"
    **Never power on a lidar that is wired with the custom connector** instead of the cable included in the box. Doing so slowly burns out the lidar over time.

    Lidars 02, 03, and 04 are likely burned out. They no longer have their original cables, so they cannot be tested. Whether the original cables can be soldered back without further intermittent issues is unknown. We recommend never plugging them in again.
