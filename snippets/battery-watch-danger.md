!!! danger "Always Watch the Battery"
    **The pilot must always watch the battery while flying.** Conservative failsafes stop you from flying on a low battery, but they do not replace watching it.

    **Leaving a drone plugged in for a long time (for example 3 hours) does not prevent the battery from draining slowly.** You can easily draw a LiPo below its safe limit of 3.0 V per cell. This permanently damages the battery, and PX4 gives no warning at all.
